Our story

A home for our people.

Beth Ammi means “house of my people”—a name that reflects our calling to be a welcoming church family rooted in faith and community.

Rev. Pastor Gatabazi Alfred established the first parish in 2007 in Nyagahinga Cell, Rusororo Sector, Gasabo District, Kigali. From that beginning, Beth Ammi has grown into a church community serving people in Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of Congo.

Beth Ammi is legally registered to operate in Rwanda under Ministerial Order No. 07/08.11 of 8 February 2010.
