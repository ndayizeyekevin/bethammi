About Women Ministries

Women Ministries at Bethammi (WMB) is a ministry working in Bethammi Christian Fellowship Church that is made of Women around Bethammi Parishes.

Each Parish organizes its activities that are mainly related with Humanitarian Activities, Spiritual and Economic development.  WMB started its activities few months after the birth of Bethammi Christian Fellowship Church in 2007. This ministry has more than 1300 women around our parishes.  At Bethammi CFC, women are one of the Church Pillars and are doing a wonderful work of God
